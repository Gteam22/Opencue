The most effective lines are **specific to the situation**, easy to answer, and do not make her feel trapped. Say the line once, pause, and see whether she turns toward you, smiles, or gives more than a minimal response.

Avoid approaching when she has headphones on, is walking quickly, is working, looks upset, or is alone somewhere isolated.

## The most reusable formula

A reliable opener has three parts:

**Observation + positive comment + small conversational opening**

Examples:

* そのTシャツ、〇〇ですよね。僕も好きなので思わず声かけました。
* すごく楽しそうですね。今日ここに来たの正解でしたね。
* そのネイル、デザインが珍しくていいですね。自分で選んだんですか？
* さっきから何回か目が合ってますよね。挨拶しようと思って来ました。
* その衣装、細かいところまで完成度高いですね。作るの大変そうです。

The best line is rarely the cleverest one. It is usually the line that proves you noticed **something specific about her or the shared situation**, rather than approaching every woman with the same script.
